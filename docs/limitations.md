# Limitations

- No-root ADB cannot access most app-private directories and databases.
- Android app sandboxing is respected; DroidForensicX does not bypass it.
- Android 16 and modern Android versions do not guarantee recoverable deleted internal files through logical ADB.
- Browser passwords are not extracted, decrypted, dumped, or bypassed.
- Social media private databases are not dumped or bypassed.
- App uninstall history is not guaranteed; traces are confidence-scored.
- Missing optional tools reduce coverage but do not stop core reporting.
- Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
