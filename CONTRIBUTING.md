# Contributing to DroidForensicX

DroidForensicX was founded and is maintained by Faysal Ferdous. Contributions are welcome under the MIT License in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Copyright and contribution rights

- Submit only work you own or are authorized to contribute under the project's MIT terms. Identify third-party material and retain its copyright notices, attribution and applicable license terms.
- The founder owns his original work and any rights validly assigned to him. Contributors otherwise retain their copyrights; a pull request, commit, or attribution is not itself a copyright assignment.
- To support centralized ownership, the maintainer proposes a prospective copyright assignment process for original contributions. See [CAA.md](CAA.md) for a draft individual agreement. Before activating that process, obtain legal review and publish the final terms. A separate signed agreement identifying the contribution is required for any assignment; do not post signatures, identity documents or private contact details in public issues or pull requests.
- No prior contributions are transferred retroactively without a valid separate agreement. Existing MIT permissions and third-party rights are unaffected.

## Review requirements

Describe your change, document relevant limitations, and include appropriate tests. Preserve authorized-use boundaries, default secret redaction, safe subprocess usage, and professional report language. Do not introduce bypass, exploitation, credential dumping, or sandbox violation logic. Use synthetic data; never commit private evidence or credentials. Run available tests and await maintainer review before merge.
